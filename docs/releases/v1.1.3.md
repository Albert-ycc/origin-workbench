# Origin Workbench v1.1.3

Origin v1.1.3 turns the meeting workspace into a recoverable recording and transcription workflow. The release focuses on practical meeting-room capture: save audio first, transcribe asynchronously when a provider is configured, repair the transcript before generating a summary, and keep long meetings from silently dropping content.

## Highlights

- Meeting audio can be saved as meeting-scoped assets, then transcribed through ASR jobs with retryable status and persisted history.
- Local Whisper and external HTTP ASR providers share a normalized segment schema. External ASR is configured through environment variables and does not store provider tokens in the UI.
- Transcript segments now support edit, delete, split, and merge operations with backend-owned sequence ordering and realtime cache updates.
- Long meeting summaries now read all transcript pages and persist chunk source ranges before producing the final review draft.
- The meeting page shows saved recordings after refresh, keeps the newest ASR job per audio asset, and surfaces repeated attempts and failure details.
- The recorder explicitly states that browser capture records the microphone only, not system audio or meeting-speaker output.

## Compatibility Notes

- Realtime browser speech transcription still falls back to manual recording when unsupported.
- Local ASR requires a configured local command, such as the provided Whisper wrapper, and a working local `whisper` installation.
- External ASR expects a multipart `file` upload response in the normalized `segments` JSON format documented in `.env.example`.
- System-audio capture, speaker diarization, meeting bot joins, calendar integration, and provider-specific external runbooks remain future platform work.
